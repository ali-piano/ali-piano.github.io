Bio About Me

I'm a concert pianist at the Cairo Opera House, and transcribing by ear is one of the things I do best. I can write out songs that have no published sheet music anywhere — accurately, with proper harmony and voice leading, not just the melody line.

Because I trained classically, the arrangements are built properly: idiomatic voicings, correct part writing, fingering-friendly notation. Not just chords under a melody.

I work across pop, jazz, indie, Disney, cinematic, Christmas, and church/worship — any genre, any instrumentation, any skill level.
